06 / First phase

QVibe begins with Kondapalli, Mangalagiri and Machilipatnam / Pedana — three living traditions connected to our first research journey from the Andhra Capital Region.

Our first responsibility is to understand their places, materials, making processes, stories, present realities and possibilities for the future.
